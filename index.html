01 · Market Overview

What the drugs are, why now, and what the real numbers say

ℹ️
All stats from NFHS-5 (Govt of India), CDSCO, Forbes India & Economic Times (2025–2026). No numbers were fabricated.

What are GLP-1 drugs?

Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) and Tirzepatide (Mounjaro) mimic a hormone that regulates appetite and blood sugar. Both are CDSCO-approved in India (semaglutide for diabetes + obesity; tirzepatide for diabetes). Both are injectable, prescription-only, Schedule H drugs.

Patent Expiry = Opportunity

Semaglutide's Indian patent expired March 2026. 40+ generics are now entering the market. Price dropped from ~₹12,800/month (innovator) to ~₹1,290/month (generics). First time the middle class can afford GLP-1 treatment.

Real Target Numbers

Per NFHS-5 (2019–21): ~16% of Indian adults are obese — ~70 million people. Urban prevalence ~40%. India has ~77 million diabetics (IDF 2023). Both are primary GLP-1 indications.

Market Size

₹1,000–1,200 Cr India GLP-1 market (2025)
₹4,500–5,000 Cr projected by 2030
13–14% CAGR global peptide market
Sources: Forbes India, Economic Times 2025

What About Research Peptides?

BPC-157, TB-500 etc. are not approved by CDSCO for human use. Selling them as medicines = criminal liability. US FDA reclassified them Feb 2026. Do not build your core business around these.

Business Model

You are a telehealth + pharmacy platform — not a drug manufacturer. Like Practo or 1mg, but specialised entirely for GLP-1 care. Connect patients with licensed doctors, issue e-prescriptions via ABDM, route orders to licensed pharmacies.

03 · Legal Risks

The landmines — know them before you step on one

CRITICAL

Selling drugs without a drug licence

Criminal offence under Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940. Penalty: up to 3 years imprisonment + fine + licence cancellation. Many platforms have been shut down for this.

Get wholesale drug licence before any drug-related feature goes live. Start with a licensed pharmacy partner while yours is pending.
CRITICAL

Research peptides sold as medical products

BPC-157 and TB-500 are NOT approved by CDSCO for human medical use. Selling them as medicines = criminal prosecution. US FDA reclassified them Feb 2026. India may follow.

Build only around CDSCO-approved drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide). Keep any peptide exploration completely separate.
HIGH

Misleading drug marketing

CDSCO and ASCI actively monitor digital ads for unapproved GLP-1 claims. Marketing as "fat burner" = enforcement action + ad takedowns.

All marketing copy reviewed by regulatory lawyer before publishing. Only claim approved indications. Mandatory disclaimers on all content.
HIGH

Unverified doctors on platform

If a non-NMC-registered person prescribes via your platform, the platform bears criminal liability too. Consumer Protection Act also applies.

Build NMC registration verification into doctor onboarding. Cross-check against NMC public registry before any doctor goes live.
MEDIUM

Patient data stored outside India

DPDP Act 2023 requires health data to remain in India. Using US-region AWS or any overseas server = violation. Penalties up to ₹250 Cr per incident.

AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) or Google Cloud Mumbai from day one. No exceptions for health records.
MEDIUM

Cold chain failure

GLP-1 injectables require 2–8°C storage end-to-end. A temperature excursion that degrades the drug and harms a patient = product liability lawsuit.

Use certified cold-chain logistics only (Delhivery Pharma, Blue Dart Pharma). Require temperature logs per shipment. Product liability insurance mandatory.

07 · Marketing Plan

Pharma marketing is restricted — here is what you can actually do

🚨
Restricted: Google and Meta restrict direct drug advertising. You cannot run ads naming specific drugs for weight loss. Promote the consultation service, not the drug. Every ad needs legal review before it goes live.

SEO Content (Best Channel)

Almost no authoritative Indian GLP-1 content exists. High-intent keywords with low competition:

  • “Ozempic cost in India 2026”
  • “Semaglutide doctor online India”
  • “Weight loss injection India side effects”
  • “How to get GLP-1 prescription India”

30 articles before launch. 4–6 months to rank. Cheapest sustainable channel.

Doctor-Led Video Content

Trust through education — the only ethical way to do pharma marketing:

  • Explainers: “How does semaglutide work?”
  • Myth-busting: “Is Ozempic safe for Indians?”
  • Anonymous patient journey stories

A real doctor on camera builds more trust than any ad spend.

Doctor Referral Network

Many GPs don't want to manage GLP-1 patients long-term:

  • Reach out to 50+ general physicians
  • Present at diabetes / endocrinology CME events
  • LinkedIn outreach to diabetologists

Highest quality patients, lowest acquisition cost.

Paid Ads (After Legal Clearance)

Only after drug licence is active and every ad has legal sign-off:

  • Google Search: “weight loss doctor online India”
  • Meta: Age 30–55, urban, health interests
  • Promote the consultation, not the drug name

Micro-Influencers

10K–500K followers convert better than celebrities for medical topics:

  • Dietitians / nutritionists on Instagram
  • Fitness coaches discussing obesity science
  • All must disclose paid partnership (ASCI rules)

Corporate Wellness (B2B)

Higher margin, recurring revenue. Target:

  • IT companies with sedentary workforces
  • Employee health insurance add-ons
  • Fertility clinics (GLP-1 + PCOS connection)

13 · Honest Verdict

No BS — here is what we actually think after researching this

Can you do this? Yes. Should you? Here's the truth.

🚫

The “peptides” your friend mentioned are illegal in India. BPC-157, TB-500 etc. are not approved by CDSCO for human medical use. Selling them as medicines = criminal prosecution under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act. If your friend is exporting them to the US, that's their risk. Do not build an Indian business on this.

👤

You cannot do this alone as a developer. The legal architecture requires a registered pharmacist, NMC-registered doctors, and a pharma regulatory consultant from Day 1. These are non-negotiable legal requirements. You need co-founders or key hires before you can legally operate.

⏳

6–9 months minimum before your first legal sale. Drug licence takes ~45 days after premises + pharmacist are ready. CDSCO product registration takes 6 months. Anyone who tells you they can launch a legal pharma platform in 2 months is misleading you.

💸

₹30–50L minimum capital required. This assumes you build all the tech yourself. If you hire anyone else for tech, add ₹30–50L more. Do you have this, or do you need to raise it? That's your first real question.

🏁

You are not jumping in before anyone else. HealthifyRx, Apollo 24/7, PharmEasy, 1mg — already in this market. HealthifyRx has a GLP-1 program running right now. The “first mover” window has passed. Execution quality and clinical credibility are your moat now.

✅

The opportunity is real and worth pursuing — with eyes open. 70 million obese Indians. 77 million diabetics. Generic semaglutide at ₹1,290/month for the first time. No dominant platform with great UX and real clinical depth yet. If you have the capital, a doctor co-founder, and patience for 9+ months before revenue — go for it. If not, fix those three things first.
