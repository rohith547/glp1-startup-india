Legal Risks

The know them before you step on them

landmines 03
CRITICAL

Selling drugs without a drug license

Criminal offense under Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940. Penalty: up to 3 years imprisonment + fine + license cancellation. Many platforms have been shut down for this.

Get wholesale drug license before any drug-related feature goes live. Start with a licensed pharmacy partner while your own license is pending.
CRITICAL

Research peptides sold as medical products

BPC-157 and TB-500 are NOT approved by CDSCO for human medical use. Selling them as medicines in India = criminal liability. The US FDA reclassified them in Feb 2026. India may follow. This is the riskiest part of what your friend described.

Do NOT build a business around unapproved research peptides. Focus only on CDSCO-approved drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide). Completely separate business if you ever explore peptides.
HIGH

Misleading drug marketing

CDSCO and Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) actively monitor digital ads for unapproved claims about GLP-1 drugs. Marketing as "fat burner" or "cosmetic weight loss" without approved indication = enforcement action + ad takedowns.

All marketing copy reviewed by your regulatory lawyer before publishing. Only claim approved indications. Mandatory disclaimers on all content.
HIGH

Unverified doctors on platform

If a non-NMC-registered person prescribes via your platform, the platform bears criminal liability alongside the individual. Consumer Protection Act also applies.

Build NMC registration number verification into doctor cross-check against the NMC public registry before any doctor goes live.
onboarding
MEDIUM

Patient data stored outside India

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 requires health data to remain in India. Using US-region AWS, Firebase, or any overseas server = violation. Penalties up Crore per incident.

to
AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) or Google Cloud Mumbai from day one. No exceptions for health records.
MEDIUM

Cold chain failure

GLP-1 injectables require storage end-to-end. A temperature excursion that degrades the drug and harms a patient = product liability lawsuit.

82
Use certified cold-chain logistics partners only (Delhivery Pharma, Blue Dart). Require temperature logs per shipment. Product liability insurance mandatory.
