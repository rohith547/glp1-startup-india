01 — Market Overview

Why GLP-1 drugs in India right now is a generational opportunity

💊 What Are GLP-1 Drugs?

GLP-1 receptor agonists (Ozempic = Semaglutide, Mounjaro = Tirzepatide) are injectable drugs originally for Type 2 diabetes, now proven to reduce body weight by 15–22%. Tirzepatide is ~2× more effective than semaglutide for weight loss. Both are now CDSCO-approved in India.

📈 Why Now — The Patent Cliff

Semaglutide's Indian patent expired March 2026. 40+ generic versions now launching. Price dropped from ₹8,800/month → ₹1,290/month. This makes treatment accessible to the Indian middle class for the first time — a tipping point moment.

🇮🇳 India's Obesity Crisis

India has 135M+ obese adults (3rd largest globally). 77M+ diabetics. Urban prevalence rising sharply. Less than 3% of eligible patients are currently treated for obesity — a massive unmet need with virtually no brand loyalty yet established.

🌐 Peptides Opportunity

Research peptides (BPC-157, TB-500) for recovery, healing, and regenerative wellness are growing fast globally. India is a major API manufacturer. However these are NOT approved for human medical use — must be positioned carefully as research/wellness products, not medicine.

💰 Market Numbers

₹1,200 Cr — India GLP-1 market (2025)
₹5,000 Cr — Projected by 2030
13–14% CAGR — Global peptide market
<3% — Treatment penetration today

🎯 Your Business Model

A specialized telehealth + digital pharmacy platform for metabolic health. Patients get doctor consult → e-prescription → generic GLP-1 delivered → ongoing monitoring. Think "Noom meets a licensed Indian pharmacy" but built for GLP-1 specifically.

03 — Legal Risks & Mitigations

Know the landmines before you step on them

HIGH

Selling without proper drug license

Operating an online pharmacy without a wholesale/retail drug license is a criminal offense under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. Penalty: imprisonment up to 3 years + fine + license cancellation.

Get wholesale drug license before any drug-related feature goes live. Partner with a pre-licensed pharmacy initially.
HIGH

Unauthorized/misleading drug marketing

CDSCO actively monitors social media and digital ads for unlicensed claims about GLP-1 drugs. Marketing as "weight loss miracle" or "fat burner" without approved indications triggers enforcement action.

All marketing copy must be reviewed by your regulatory lawyer. Only claim CDSCO-approved indications. No before/after imagery without medical disclaimers.
HIGH

Non-NMC doctor consultations

If an unregistered practitioner prescribes on your platform, both the doctor and the platform face criminal liability under the Indian Medical Council Act and Consumer Protection Act.

Build NMC registration verification API into your onboarding flow. No doctor goes live without verified NMC number cross-checked against the official registry.
MEDIUM

Patient data breach (DPDP Act 2023)

Health data is "sensitive personal data." A breach or storing data outside India violates the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Penalties up to ₹250 Crore per incident.

Host all data on Indian servers (AWS Mumbai, Google Cloud Mumbai). Implement encryption at rest and in transit. Hire a Data Protection Officer (DPO) from day one.
MEDIUM

Regulatory reclassification of peptides

Research peptides (BPC-157, TB-500) could be reclassified as prescription drugs at any point. FDA already moved on this in Feb 2026. India CDSCO may follow.

Do NOT position research peptides as medical products. Keep this line of business completely separate and have an exit clause ready in any partnerships.
MEDIUM

Cold chain logistics failure

GLP-1 injectables require 2–8°C storage throughout the supply chain. A temperature excursion that degrades the drug and harms a patient creates product liability lawsuits.

Use only certified cold-chain logistics partners (Delhivery Pharma, Blue Dart). Require temperature monitoring reports on every shipment. Product liability insurance is mandatory.
LOW

GST and tax compliance

Pharma products have varied GST rates. Incorrect classification creates audit risk.

Hire a CA with pharma e-commerce experience. GLP-1 drugs are typically 12% GST; consult services are 18%.
